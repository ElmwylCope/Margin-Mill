The problem

More reports won't solve a prioritization problem.

Most distributors already have financial statements. What's often missing is a simple view of where profit performance is deteriorating, how significant it may be, and which questions management should examine first.

01 / GROSS PROFIT

Sales grow. Profit doesn't keep up.

Revenue can rise while gross profit growth lags. A Profit Map shows whether that gap exists, how it's trending, and what the change may represent in dollars.

02 / COST STRUCTURE

Overhead absorbs the gain.

Expenses creep upward and operating leverage weakens. We compare expense movement with revenue and gross profit—not just last month's budget.

03 / FOCUS

Everything looks urgent.

A long list of possible fixes isn't a plan. We narrow the initial read to the two areas that appear to merit the most attention and validation.

What the Profit Map is—and isn't.

It identifies financial patterns and estimates potential opportunity size using client-provided statements. Monthly P&Ls alone cannot prove the root cause of pricing leakage, customer mix changes, purchasing decisions, or inventory and cash-cycle problems. Transaction-level investigation and implementation are separate, optional work. Findings are decision support, not audit, tax, legal or CPA services; no specific result is guaranteed.

Common questions

Know what you're getting.

A defined first look—without a vague consulting pitch or a required meeting to start.

Is the Profit Map really free?

Yes. The initial Profit Map is provided at no cost to accepted, eligible companies. There is no obligation to purchase anything. If you'd like help investigating or implementing a finding later, that would be a separate, optional engagement.

Do I have to schedule a sales call?

No. We can confirm fit, gather the data, clarify questions and send the completed Profit Map by email. A follow-up conversation is available if you'd like to discuss the findings, but it is not a prerequisite to request one.

What financial information will you need?

Start with your last 12 months of profit-and-loss statements broken out by month, ideally exported from your accounting or ERP system. A recent balance sheet may add context but isn't required for the core P&L-based review. Please don't upload financial statements through this public form.

Will it show exactly why our margin changed?

Not necessarily. A P&L can reveal that margin or operating expenses changed and estimate the financial significance. Explaining the root cause usually requires further detail such as customer, product, invoice, purchasing or freight records. We'll distinguish observations from hypotheses.

How will you handle our financial information?

The request form collects only basic contact and fit information. We'll coordinate a suitable transfer process for financial statements after confirming fit. We won't publish company-identifying information, specific results or testimonials without separate written permission. Never submit bank credentials, Social Security numbers, or employee-level personal information.

How long does delivery take?

We typically aim for about five business days after accepting the request and receiving usable data. Timing can vary with workload, reporting format and questions that need clarification; it's not a guaranteed deadline.
